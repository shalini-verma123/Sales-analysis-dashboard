# Sales-analysis-dashboard
Sales analysis dashboard using power BI

## Project Overview
The Super Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, payment modes, and shipping methods to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization
- DAX / Power BI Measures (if used in the project)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** 522.44K
- **Total Profit:** 67.86K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 7K
- **Average Value:** 3.89 (as displayed on the dashboard)

## Dashboard Features
- **Sales and Profit Analysis:** Tracks overall sales and profit performance.
- **Regional Analysis:** Provides a region filter to explore sales performance by region.
- **Payment Mode Analysis:** Visualizes sales by payment methods such as COD, online payments, and cards.
- **Customer Segment Analysis:** Compares sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- **Monthly Sales and Profit Trends:** Shows year-over-year trends for 2019 and 2020.
- **Shipping Mode Analysis:** Compares sales across shipping methods.
- **Category and Sub-Category Analysis:** Displays sales across product categories and sub-categories.
- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Examines sales over time using order dates.

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached approximately 522.44K, with total profit of 67.86K.
- The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales among the displayed customer segments.
- Standard Class is the leading shipping mode by sales in the dashboard.
- Office Supplies is the leading product category by sales among the displayed categories.
- The dashboard allows users to explore sales trends over time and compare performance across different business dimensions.
